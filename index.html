What We Do

Six disciplines, one accountable team

From statutory audit through to trust administration, our work is built to give you timely, relevant financial information — and the confidence to act on it.

  1. 01

    Auditing & Assurance

    An independent audit service designed to enhance the reliability of your financial statements, alongside consulting on the frameworks that govern public sector reporting.

    • Statutory audits
    • Independent reviews
    • Quality & pre-issuance reviews
    • Assurance consulting
  2. 02

    Accounting & Financial Services

    The provision of timely and relevant financial information — so the numbers you make decisions on are current, reconciled and understood.

    • Monthly accounting
    • Annual financial statements
    • Management reporting
    • Payroll support
  3. 03

    Taxation

    Completion and submission of returns, and dealing with SARS on your behalf — including queries, objections, and the follow-up of fines and interest.

    • Income Tax, VAT, PAYE & UIF
    • Provisional tax returns
    • SARS queries & objections
    • Tax directives
    • Tax clearance certificates
  4. 04

    Secretarial Services

    Registration and ongoing statutory maintenance of companies and trusts, keeping your entity compliant and its records in order.

    • Company & trust registration
    • Share issues & transfers
    • Special resolutions & minutes
    • Share register maintenance
    • Registered address services
    • Changes to directors & members
  5. 05

    Trusts & Estates

    Formation and administration of trusts, together with estate planning and administration — structured with the next generation in mind.

    • Trust formation
    • Trust administration
    • Estate planning
    • Estate administration
  6. 06

    Audit Training

    Audit training and workshops, plus on-the-job consulting and training — a service we offer because we invest in technical development as a matter of course.

    • Audit standards
    • Auditing in the public sector
    • Customised courses
    • On-the-job training

Public Sector

Fluent in the frameworks that govern public money

Public sector engagements carry their own rulebook, and it is not the one that applies to a private company. We consult across the standards and regulations that departments, municipalities and public entities are measured against — and we train other auditors in them.

Private sector clients get the same team and the same technical standard, applied to the frameworks that govern them.

GRAP

Generally Recognised Accounting Practice — the reporting framework for public sector entities.

PFMA

Public Finance Management Act compliance and the controls it requires.

Predetermined Objectives

Auditing reported performance information against predetermined objectives.

Supply Chain Management

SCM processes, procurement compliance and the findings that follow from them.

Treasury Regulations

Applying National Treasury regulations and instruction notes in practice.

Audit Training

Workshops and customised courses on auditing in the public sector.

About the Firm

A quarter-century of practice, still built around the client in front of us.

RMS Chartered Accountants Inc. is a dynamic firm in the public and private sector, delivering a professional and personal service with ethical values.

We invest in our employees' personal and technical development, because that is what allows us to deliver a unique and quality service to our clients. It is also why audit training sits alongside our client work rather than apart from it.
